2. What the service is

DigiMenu turns your menu into a page your guests open by scanning a QR code or by following your link, and — on plans that include ordering — lets them place an order from that page. Orders reach you as a WhatsApp message and are recorded in your dashboard.

The service runs in a web browser. We do not supply hardware, a printed menu, a QR stand or a point-of-sale system, and we are not a payment processor: guests do not pay through DigiMenu, and we take no commission on any order.

We provide the service as it exists on the day you use it. We improve it continuously, and a feature may change or be withdrawn — if a change materially reduces what you paid for, you may cancel and we will refund the unused part of the period as described in clause 10.

3. Your account

You need an account to publish a menu. You must give accurate details for the restaurant, keep the sign-in credentials secure, and tell us promptly if you believe someone else has access. Anything done through your account is treated as done by you.

You may invite staff as members of your account. You are responsible for what they do, and for removing their access when they leave. One account covers one restaurant unless we agree otherwise in writing.

You must be at least 18 years old and authorised to act for the business you register.

4. The free week

A new account gets one free week from the day it is created. Nothing is charged at sign-up and no card is taken. The free week applies once per business: closing an account to open another for the same restaurant does not start a second one.

During the free week you can build and publish the full menu. When it ends, the menu stays published for a short grace period while we ask you to choose a plan; if no plan is chosen it is paused, not deleted, and resumes the day you pay.

5. Plans, prices and payment

Our plans and their prices are set out on our pricing page. The current plans are:

  • QR Menu — AED 99 per year, up to 50 menu items, no ordering.
  • Pro — AED 999 per year or AED 129 per month, with dine-in, take-away and delivery ordering.
  • Ultra — AED 1,999 per year or AED 249 per month, adding a branded landing page. A custom domain is an optional add-on at AED 99 per year.

Prices are in United Arab Emirates dirhams and include 5% VAT. We may change prices; a change never applies to a period you have already paid for, and we will tell you before it applies to you.

Payment is by bank transfer or arranged over WhatsApp, and your menu is activated when the payment is received, normally the same working day. Nothing renews automatically and we hold no card details. If a period is not renewed, the service pauses at the end of the period you paid for.
