How it works

Four steps, one afternoon

You do the first three once. After that, changing a price or marking a dish sold out takes a few seconds from your phone.

1

Claim your link

Search your restaurant name. If it is free, it becomes yours at ogle.live/digimenu/your-name and we generate the QR code from that address.

The QR downloads as a PNG from your dashboard — print it on the table tent, stick it on the window, put it in the takeaway bag.
2

Add your logo and licence

Your logo and trade licence copy are what we need before the menu goes public. A Google Maps link is optional — it only fills in your address and hours.

You can build and preview the whole menu while it is unpublished. Nothing is public until you have added both documents.
3

Bring in the menu

Upload a photo or a PDF of the printed menu, or paste your Talabat or Deliveroo link. We read the categories, dishes and prices, and show you every line to correct before anything goes live.

Combos, half and full portions, dishes without a price: we flag anything we are unsure about instead of guessing.
4

Take the order

The guest picks dishes and chooses dine-in, takeaway or delivery. We open WhatsApp on their phone with the full order typed out — their table number, their pickup time, their address.

The same order is emailed to you and kept in your dashboard as a record.
Features

Built around how a kitchen actually works

No printer, no tablet, no new device behind the counter. The menu lives on the guest's phone and the order arrives where your staff already are.

Reads a photo of your menu

One page or six, printed or photographed. Categories, dish names, descriptions and prices are pulled out line by line for you to approve.

Nothing publishes without your review.

Imports from Talabat or Deliveroo

Already listed on a delivery platform? Paste the link to your page and we take the menu and dish photos from there instead of you retyping it.

Falls back to a photo upload if the page cannot be read.

Sold out in one tap

Run out of a dish at 9pm? Switch it off from your phone. It stays in the menu, greyed out and unorderable, and comes back when you switch it back on.

No republishing, no designer.

Orders to WhatsApp, copied to email

The guest sends the order from their own WhatsApp to your number — no new app, no login, no Meta account to set up. We email you the same order and keep it in your dashboard.

Your staff reply and confirm as they always have.

QR and short link included

Every menu gets a QR code generated from its own address. Download the PNG any time from the dashboard and reprint it for a new branch.

The address never changes once claimed.

Your menu stays yours

Every menu, logo and order is stored against your account, so you have a record of what you sold and when. Export the menu as a spreadsheet whenever you want it.

Your data is not sold or shared.
