The whole restaurant side is one screen.

The menu your guests see is the menu you edit. Change a price, add a dish, or take something off at 9pm because the kitchen has run out, and the guest page is already correct.

  • Sold out in one tap. The dish stays visible, struck through, and cannot be ordered.
  • Every order is kept. Recorded with its table, time and total, and emailed to you.
  • Your QR, download ready. One address, one code, printable for every table at once.

From a photo to the first order.

01

Claim your address

Search your restaurant name. If it is free it becomes yours at ogle.live/digimenu/your-name, and the QR is generated from that address.

The address never changes once claimed, so a printed table card stays valid.
02

Add your logo and licence

Two documents and the menu can go public. You can build the whole thing, and preview it, before either is uploaded.

A Google Maps link is optional. It only fills in your address and hours.
03

Bring the menu in

A photo, a PDF, a spreadsheet, or the link to your page on a delivery platform. We read the categories, dishes and prices and show you every line to correct.

Anything under 90% confidence is flagged rather than guessed. Nothing publishes without your review.
04

Take the order

The guest picks dishes and chooses dine-in, takeaway or delivery. WhatsApp opens on their phone with the order already written: table number, pickup time or address included.

The same order is emailed to the restaurant and kept in your dashboard.

Run out at nine o'clock.
