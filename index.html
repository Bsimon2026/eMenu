Three steps

From the menu you have to the order in your hand, the same afternoon.

Nothing to install, nothing to print again. The menu you build is the menu your guests see, and the one you edit tomorrow morning.

01 — Bring the menu in

You already own the content. We do the typing.

Send a photo of your printed menu, a PDF, a spreadsheet, or the link to your page on a delivery platform. We read the categories, dishes, descriptions and prices, then show you every line to correct before anything goes live.

  • 114 dishes in the demo menu were read from one link
  • Price, description and photo, editable line by line
  • Nothing is saved until you approve it
Photo · PDF · Excel · link
02 — Put the code on the table

Guests scan once. No app, no download, no sign-up.

A QR code and a permanent link, printed on the table card, the window or the takeaway bag. The menu opens in the browser with the photos, today's sold-out switches and both languages — English and Arabic, from the same menu.

  • Sold-out in one tap — the dish greys out on every phone at once
  • Change a price and it is live before you put the phone down
  • Dine in, take away or delivery — the guest chooses
Scan · browse · order
03 — The order arrives

Every order lands in your WhatsApp, in the thread your staff already read.

No new screen for the kitchen to learn. The message carries the table, the dishes, the total and the reference number; the dashboard keeps the record, the history by date and whether the order was taken.

  • Guest gets a reply in the same thread — accepted, ready, on its way
  • Connect your own WhatsApp Business number, or ours
  • Every message logged, so nothing is answered twice

Questions

The things restaurants ask before paying.

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. They scan the code and the menu opens in the browser — no app, no account, no download. The same link works from a WhatsApp message, a Google search or the sticker on the table.

What happens when I go past 50 dishes on QR Menu?

The QR plan holds 50 dishes and a photo on each. If you outgrow it, the dashboard tells you before you hit the limit and offers Pro, which has no dish count. Nothing is deleted while you decide.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

No. Orders reach the number you already use. If you want the automated replies and the green tick, Pro and Ultra can connect your own WhatsApp Business number through the Cloud API — Meta bills per message for that, separately from us.

Is the menu really in Arabic too?

Yes, on every plan. Each dish takes an Arabic name beside its English one, and the guest switches the whole page.

Can I put it on my own domain?

On Ultra, as an add-on of AED 99 a year. Your domain points at your menu, and your existing website — if you have one — keeps working.

What if I stop paying?

The QR keeps working until the period you paid for ends. After that the page pauses rather than disappears, and the menu comes back the moment you renew. Nothing is deleted, and nothing renews without you asking.
