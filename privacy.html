Privacy Policy

Ogle Live Technology, Dubai, United Arab Emirates · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers two different people: the restaurant that uses DigiMenu, and the guest who orders from a restaurant's menu. It says what we hold about each of them, why, where it lives, how long it stays, and how to have it corrected or removed.

1. Who is responsible

Ogle Live Technology, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, is the controller of the data described here for the accounts and websites we run. For the guest data that passes through a restaurant's menu — names, phone numbers and orders — the restaurant is the controller and we are its processor: we handle that data on the restaurant's instructions and for no other purpose.

We handle personal data in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the “PDPL”). Questions and requests: connect@indubaicity.com.

2. What we collect

If you run a restaurant on DigiMenu

  • Account — name, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and the staff members you invite.
  • Restaurant profile — trading name, handle, city, contact and order numbers, email, address, logo, opening details, social links, and the appearance you choose.
  • Menu content — categories, dishes, prices, descriptions, photographs, translations and sold-out switches.
  • Operations — orders received, messages sent and received, review requests, discount codes and their redemptions.
  • Billing state — plan, the period you have paid for, amount and reference of the transfer. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical — the IP address and user agent recorded by our host when you sign in, kept for security and abuse prevention.

If you are a guest ordering from a menu

  • What you type — the name and phone number you give for the order, the mode (dine in, take away, delivery), the table or address where relevant, the items and the total, and any note you add.
  • WhatsApp — if you message the restaurant, we store the message and your number so the restaurant can reply in one thread.
  • Reviews — the rating and comment you leave through a review link, and whether you chose to go on to Google.

We do not ask for, and do not want, payment card details, identity documents, health information or anything else sensitive. A menu page never asks a guest to create an account.

3. Why we hold it

Under the PDPL we rely on the following grounds:

  • Performing our contract with you — running your account, publishing your menu, delivering orders and keeping the record of them that your dashboard shows.
  • Your consent — asking a guest for a review, and any optional message you choose to send.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fraud, and understanding which parts of the product are used so we can improve them. We weigh these against your rights and keep the data to what is needed.
  • Legal obligation — retaining billing records where UAE tax law requires it, and answering a lawful request from an authority.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use guest data for our own marketing.

4. Guests and restaurants

When a guest orders, their details go to the restaurant whose menu they used, and to us as the operator of that page. The restaurant decides what else it does with them — its own marketing, for example — and its privacy notice applies to that. Our part is limited to publishing the menu, delivering the order, storing the record and sending the messages you see in the thread.

If you are a guest and want your order removed, the fastest route is to ask the restaurant. You may also write to us and we will act on the restaurant's instruction, or on your own request where the law allows us to.

5. Who else touches it

We use a small number of service providers, each under a contract that limits them to processing on our instructions:

  • Vercel — serves the website and runs the API.
  • Supabase — the database and authentication, with row-level security so one restaurant's records cannot be read from another's.
  • Meta / WhatsApp — delivers the messages a restaurant sends to its guests, where the Cloud API is connected.
  • DeepSeek — reads a menu photograph or link you upload and returns the categories and dishes. Only the file or link you submit is sent; no guest data is included.
  • Google — only when a guest chooses to continue to the restaurant's Google review page.
  • Our email provider — sends order copies and account emails.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers. What we do store:

  • A sign-in token for your dashboard, kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.
  • Your own preferences — the menu you were last editing, and similar settings — kept locally on your device.
  • A guest's cart — held in the browser of the guest, not sent to us until the order is placed.

Clearing your browser storage signs you out and empties a guest cart; nothing else is affected.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and menu — while the account is open, and for 30 days after it is closed so a return is possible. Then deleted.
  • Orders and messages — kept so a restaurant can look back at its own history; the restaurant can archive or delete them, and we remove them when the account is closed.
  • Review requests — kept with the order they belong to.
  • Billing records — kept for the period UAE tax law requires.
  • Security logs — 90 days.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, data at rest is encrypted by our providers, access to the database requires an authenticated session, and one restaurant's records are isolated from another's at the database level, not only in the code. Staff access is limited to those who need it.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the PDPL requires, and tell you what we are doing about it.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPL you may ask us to:

  • confirm what we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete it, where there is no legal reason for us to keep it;
  • restrict or object to a particular use, including our legitimate-interest processing;
  • give you your data in a portable form; and
  • withdraw a consent you gave earlier, such as review requests.

Write to connect@indubaicity.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first. If you are not satisfied with our answer you may complain to the UAE Data Office.

10. Where data is stored

Our database and hosting are provided by companies that may store data outside the UAE, principally in the European Union and the United States. Where data leaves the UAE we rely on the transfer mechanisms those providers offer and keep the same protections in place. The PDPL permits this where an appropriate safeguard exists; tell us if you need the detail for your own records.

11. Children

DigiMenu is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly hold data about children, except where a restaurant's own order contains a name for a children's meal — which the restaurant controls and we simply carry to the kitchen.

12. Changes

If we change how we handle personal data we update this page and its date. A material change is announced to account holders by email or WhatsApp at least 14 days before it takes effect.
